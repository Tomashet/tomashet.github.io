Reading Guide

RL & Safe RL

A structured path from reinforcement-learning foundations to safety under nonstationarity and adaptive AI safety.

How to Use This Guide

This is a curated learning path for students and researchers moving from reinforcement-learning foundations toward safe reinforcement learning, continual adaptation, and safety under nonstationarity. It is not an exhaustive bibliography: the emphasis is on what to read, why it matters, and where it fits.

Where a strong overview exists, each stage begins with Start with a survey. The remaining readings are divided into essential works and deeper or more specialized material. Books are collected separately because they are best used as durable foundations and references rather than read in the same way as research papers.

Recommended Books

Core texts and references

Core book · RL · Read substantially

Sutton & Barto — Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction, 2nd ed.↗

The standard starting point for RL: MDPs, value functions, dynamic programming, temporal-difference learning, policy methods, and function approximation.

Core book · CMDPs · Read relevant chapters

Eitan Altman — Constrained Markov Decision Processes↗

The mathematical foundation for constrained sequential decision-making and an important bridge from classical MDPs to constrained and safe RL.

Foundation · MDPs · Reference

Martin L. Puterman — Markov Decision Processes: Discrete Stochastic Dynamic Programming↗

A deeper theoretical reference for Markov decision processes, optimality, policies, and dynamic programming.

Advanced · Control & DP · Reference

Dimitri P. Bertsekas — Dynamic Programming and Optimal Control↗

A rigorous bridge among sequential optimization, control, dynamic programming, and ideas underlying reinforcement learning.

Classic · Approximate DP · Reference

Bertsekas & Tsitsiklis — Neuro-Dynamic Programming↗

A classic treatment of approximation, dynamic programming, and learning that helps connect classical control ideas to modern RL.

Supporting foundation · Optimization

Boyd & Vandenberghe — Convex Optimization↗

A useful mathematical reference for constrained optimization, duality, and optimization concepts that recur throughout constrained policy learning.

The labels “read substantially,” “read relevant chapters,” and “reference” are intentional: the goal is not to require every book to be read cover-to-cover.

01
Learning stage

Foundations of Reinforcement Learning

Build the vocabulary needed for everything that follows: MDPs, value functions, Bellman equations, temporal-difference learning, policy optimization, deep RL, and probabilistic views of control.

Orientation

Begin with Sutton & Barto

Use the core book above as the principal orientation source for this section; then move to representative deep-RL and policy-optimization papers.

Essential

Mnih et al. — Human-level Control through Deep Reinforcement Learning↗

The DQN paper: a natural bridge from classical value-based RL to deep function approximation.

Essential

Schulman et al. — Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms↗

A compact introduction to one of the most influential modern policy-optimization approaches.

Recommended

Haarnoja et al. — Soft Actor-Critic↗

An important off-policy actor-critic method built around maximum-entropy reinforcement learning.

Advanced

Levine — Reinforcement Learning and Control as Probabilistic Inference: Tutorial and Review↗

A valuable alternative perspective connecting reinforcement learning, control, and probabilistic inference.

02
Learning stage

From RL to Safe RL

Introduce the central change in perspective: reward maximization is no longer sufficient when behavior must also satisfy explicit safety requirements.

Start with surveys · Essential

García & Fernández — A Comprehensive Survey on Safe Reinforcement Learning↗

A foundational taxonomy of Safe RL and a strong first map of the field.

Survey · Essential

Gu et al. — modern review of Safe Reinforcement Learning↗

A newer complement emphasizing major Safe-RL problems, methods, theory, and practical applications.

Essential

Achiam et al. — Constrained Policy Optimization↗

A central paper for understanding policy optimization subject to safety constraints.

Essential

Perkins & Barto — Lyapunov Design for Safe Reinforcement Learning↗

An early and influential connection between Lyapunov reasoning and safety in reinforcement learning.

Essential

Dalal et al. — Safe Exploration in Continuous Action Spaces↗

Introduces a safety-layer perspective for enforcing constraints while an RL agent explores.

Recommended

Wachi & Sui — Safe RL in Constrained Markov Decision Processes↗

A useful CMDP-centered treatment connecting formal constraints to learning.

03
Learning stage

Safe Reinforcement Learning

Go deeper into how safety is represented and enforced: constraint formulations, policy optimization, safe exploration, shielding, runtime intervention, and control-theoretic approaches.

Start with a survey · Essential

Wachi et al. — survey of constraint formulations in Safe RL↗

Particularly useful before comparing algorithms because it focuses attention on a prior question: what exactly is the safety constraint?

Survey · Recommended

Liu et al. — survey of constrained policy learning in model-free RL↗

A focused orientation to constrained policy-optimization methods.

Survey · Recommended

Brunke et al. — Safe Learning in Robotics↗

A complementary control and robotics perspective on learning-based safety.

Essential

Berkenkamp et al. — Safe Model-based Reinforcement Learning with Stability Guarantees↗

Representative work on learning while explicitly restricting exploration to regions considered safe.

Recommended

Chow et al. — Lyapunov-based Safe Policy Optimization↗

Extends Lyapunov ideas into modern constrained policy optimization.

Recommended

Feasible Actor-Critic — constrained RL for state-wise safety↗

Useful for distinguishing cumulative constraints from safety requirements that must hold at individual states or decisions.

Advanced

Carr et al. — shielding under partial observability↗

Connects runtime shielding with uncertainty about the true system state.

Advanced

Khattar et al. — meta-safe reinforcement learning↗

A bridge toward adaptation: how safety can be considered when an agent must adapt across tasks.

04
Learning stage

Continual RL & Nonstationarity

Temporarily separate adaptation from safety. First understand what changing tasks, dynamics, observations, and contexts do to a learning system.

Start with surveys · Essential

Padakandla — survey of RL algorithms for dynamically varying environments↗

An orientation to reinforcement learning when the environment itself changes over time.

Survey · Essential

Khetarpal et al. — Towards Continual Reinforcement Learning: A Review and Perspectives↗

A broad conceptual map of continual reinforcement learning and the challenges of persistent adaptation.

Survey · Recommended

Gama et al. — A Survey on Concept Drift Adaptation↗

Broader than RL, but valuable for understanding nonstationarity and changing data-generating processes.

Essential

Al-Shedivat et al. — Continuous Adaptation via Meta-Learning in Nonstationary and Competitive Environments↗

Shows how meta-learning can support ongoing adaptation in changing environments.

Essential

Rakelly et al. — PEARL↗

Probabilistic context variables provide an important conceptual bridge to context inference and adaptation.

Recommended

Nagabandi et al. — meta-RL for adaptation in dynamic environments↗

Useful for understanding adaptation of learned dynamics and control under environmental change.

Recommended

Nagabandi, Finn & Levine — continual adaptation for model-based RL↗

Connects online learning, meta-learning, and persistent model adaptation.

Advanced

Eysenbach et al. — off-dynamics reinforcement learning↗

A useful perspective on transfer when deployment dynamics differ from training dynamics.

05
Learning stage

Safety under Nonstationarity

Bring the two threads together. The key question is no longer only whether the policy can adapt, but whether the safety specification and safety mechanism remain valid while adaptation occurs.

Start with a survey · Essential

Tomashevskiy — Safe Online Reinforcement Learning Methods in Nonstationary Environments↗

A synthesis of Safe RL, continual learning, and control perspectives, with particular attention to static, adaptive, and context-dependent safety constraints under changing conditions.

Essential

Chen et al. — context-aware Safe RL for nonstationary environments↗

Representative work connecting environmental context with safety adaptation.

Essential

Chandak et al. — safe policy improvement for nonstationary MDPs↗

Addresses policy improvement when the decision process changes over time.

Essential

Ding et al. — Safe RL in episodic CMDPs with nonstationary objectives and constraints↗

Important for understanding explicitly changing constrained decision problems.

Advanced

Wei et al. — model-free algorithms for nonstationary CMDPs↗

A theoretical perspective on efficient learning when both the environment and constraints may vary.

Advanced

Zeng et al. — online primal-dual natural actor-critic for CMDPs↗

Useful for readers interested in finite-time analysis and online constrained optimization.

Recommended

Wen et al. — robustness and safety for pre-adaptation in meta-RL↗

Connects safety with the period before and during adaptation.

Recommended

Yuan et al. — all-time safety for online safe meta-RL↗

Examines safety throughout online adaptation rather than only after convergence.

Recommended

Luo et al. — MESA↗

An offline meta-RL approach to safe adaptation and fault tolerance.

06
Learning stage

Broader AI & Agent Safety

A selective bridge beyond Safe RL. These readings connect sequential decision-making to broader questions of runtime assurance, monitoring, robustness, agent safety, and multi-agent systems.

Essential

Amodei et al. — Concrete Problems in AI Safety↗

An influential framing of practical safety problems that arise as learning systems become more capable and autonomous.

Advanced

Everitt — Towards Safe Artificial General Intelligence↗

A thesis-level treatment useful for connecting RL-style agents to broader AI-safety questions.

Recommended

SOTER — runtime assurance for safe robotic systems↗

A useful runtime-safety perspective: learned behavior can be wrapped by an independent assurance mechanism.

Advanced

Bartocci et al. — Introduction to Runtime Verification↗

Background for readers interested in monitoring properties during execution rather than relying only on design-time verification.

Survey · Recommended

Gronauer & Diepold — multi-agent deep reinforcement learning survey↗

A bridge toward endogenous nonstationarity created by other learning agents.

Survey · Recommended

Schwalbe & Schels — safety assurance of machine-learning-based systems↗

Connects learning-system safety with the broader assurance and safety-engineering perspective.

A Note on Scope

The guide intentionally becomes more selective as it moves outward from RL and Safe RL. It is designed as a path into adaptive AI safety, not as a comprehensive catalogue of reinforcement learning or AI-safety literature. The selections will evolve as the literature develops and as stronger pedagogical routes become clear.
