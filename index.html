Adaptive AI Safety

Toward AI safety as a capability maintained throughout operation,
not merely a property specified at design time.

My research focuses on AI safety for adaptive and autonomous systems that learn and operate in changing environments. I study how safety constraints can remain valid as operating conditions evolve, how emerging risks can be anticipated before they lead to unsafe behavior, and how runtime safety mechanisms can intervene when reliable adaptation is not possible.

My goal is to develop methods and principles that make adaptation itself a subject of safety assurance — enabling intelligent systems to remain safe as the world they operate in continues to change.
