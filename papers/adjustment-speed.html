Adjustment Speed as a Safety Constraint for Nonstationary Reinforcement Learning

Timothy Tomashevskiy
arXiv preprint · 2026
Publication / preprint date: July 21, 2026

Summary

Frames adaptation feasibility as a safety problem by comparing the rate of environmental change with an agent's effective adaptation capacity and proactively tightening behavior when safe adaptation may be too slow.
